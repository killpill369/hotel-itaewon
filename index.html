Hotel Itaewon sits on Bagaicha Line — rooms, a full kitchen, a bar, and a hall under one roof. Guests come for the food and end up booking the room.

01 · Stay

Rooms that actually rest you

Cool AC, crisp linen, a television, and a door that shuts out the street. Built for friends, couples, and anyone in town for the night.

02 · Kitchen

The Food table

Comforting flavours of Korean cuisine, the rich spices of Indian cuisine, familiar tastes of Continental dishes — all prepared with care and served fresh from our kitchen.
